Effective: 12 November 2026

English

In short: the game has no server of its own. It asks for no account, no name and no e-mail. Your best score, level and settings are stored only on your device.

Only Google services receive data:

  • Google AdMob (ads). An interstitial ad may appear between shifts, and you can choose to watch a rewarded ad for an extra heart. AdMob uses the advertising ID, device information and approximate location derived from the IP address to serve, measure and protect ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland a consent form is shown first; if you decline, ads are not personalised. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Google Play Billing (optional purchase). "No ads" is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details; the game only learns whether the purchase exists.

No analytics, no crash reporting, no notifications, no microphone, no camera, no location permission. If this ever changes, this policy is updated first.

Children: the game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Deletion: uninstalling the game deletes everything it stored on the device. For questions or requests, write to the address above.

How Google processes data: https://policies.google.com/privacy
